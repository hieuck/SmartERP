## 📋 Demo Script

**Opening:**
> "Thank you for your time today! I'm excited to show you how Smart ERP can help [Company Name] streamline operations and grow your business."

**Agenda:**
> "In the next 15 minutes, I'll show you:
> 1. How Smart ERP gives you real-time visibility into your business
> 2. How it simplifies your daily operations
> 3. How it helps you make better decisions with powerful reporting
>

**Discovery Questions:**

> - Pending payments: 450 million VND
>

**2. Sales Chart**
> "This chart shows your sales trend over the last 30 days. You can see we had a spike here on the 15th - that was a large order from [Customer Name].
>

**3. Revenue by Category**

### Part 2: Sales Process - From Order to Payment (5 minutes)

**Navigate to Orders > New Order**

**Key Message:**
> "Let me walk you through a typical sales order - from creation to payment. This is what your sales team does every day."

**Step 1: Create Order**
> "Let's create an order for [Customer Name]. I'll start typing their name... and the system auto-suggests. Click to select.
> 
> Notice it automatically loads their information - address, payment terms, credit limit. No need to look it up."

**Step 2: Add Products**
> "Now I'll add products. I can search by name or SKU. Let's add [Product Name]...
> 
> See how it shows the current stock level? 150 units available. This prevents overselling.
> 
> I'll add 10 units... and the system automatically calculates the total. Let me add another product..."

**Step 3: Review & Confirm**
> "Here's our order summary:
> - Subtotal: 15 million VND
> - Tax (10%): 1.5 million VND
> - Total: 16.5 million VND
> 
> I can add a discount if needed, or notes for the warehouse team.
> 
> Now I'll confirm the order... Done! Order #1234 is created."

**Step 4: Generate Invoice**
> "The system automatically generates an invoice. I can send it to the customer right now via email...
> 
> Or I can print it, export to PDF, whatever you need."

**Step 5: Record Payment**
> "When the customer pays, I click 'Record Payment'...
> 
> Select payment method - cash, bank transfer, card, e-wallet...
> 
> Enter the amount... and done! Payment recorded.
> 
> Notice the order status changed to 'Paid' and inventory was automatically reduced."

**Key Benefits:**
> "What you just saw takes about 2 minutes. Compare that to:
> - Writing orders in a notebook
> - Creating invoices in Word
> - Updating inventory in Excel
> - Recording payments separately
> 
> Smart ERP does it all in one place, automatically."

**Interaction:**
> "How long does this process take you currently?"

**Transition:**
> "Now let me show you the inventory management..."

### Part 3: Inventory Management (3 minutes)

**Navigate to Inventory > Stock Levels**

**Key Message:**
> "Inventory is the heart of your business. Let me show you how Smart ERP gives you complete control."

**1. Real-Time Stock Levels**
> "Here you can see stock levels across all warehouses in real-time.
> 
> See this product in red? That's a low stock alert. It's below the minimum threshold of 50 units.
> 
> You can set these thresholds for each product, and the system will alert you automatically."

**2. Multi-Warehouse Support**
> "If you have multiple locations, you can see stock at each warehouse.
> 
> For example, [Product Name]:
> - Main warehouse: 150 units
> - Branch 1: 45 units
> - Branch 2: 80 units
> - Total: 275 units"

**3. Stock Movements**
> "Click on any product to see its movement history...
> 
> Here you can see every transaction:
> - When it came in (purchase orders)
> - When it went out (sales orders)
> - Transfers between warehouses
> - Adjustments
> 
> Complete audit trail with who did what and when."

**4. Transfer Between Warehouses**
> "Need to move stock? Click 'Transfer'...
> 
> Select source and destination warehouses, products, quantities... done!
> 
> The system updates both locations automatically."

**Key Benefits:**
> "No more:
> - Calling warehouses to check stock
> - Spreadsheets that are out of date
> - Overselling products you don't have
> - Losing track of inventory
> 
> Everything is real-time and accurate."

**Interaction:**
> "How do you currently track inventory across locations?"

**Transition:**
> "Let me show you the reporting capabilities..."

## 💡 Demo Tips

### Do's
- ✅ Ask questions and listen
- ✅ Tailor demo to their needs
- ✅ Use their industry terminology
- ✅ Show real scenarios
- ✅ Be enthusiastic
- ✅ Handle objections confidently
- ✅ Focus on benefits, not features
- ✅ Keep it conversational

### Don'ts
- ❌ Rush through the demo
- ❌ Show every feature
- ❌ Use technical jargon
- ❌ Criticize competitors
- ❌ Make promises you can't keep
- ❌ Ignore questions
- ❌ Go over time
- ❌ Forget to ask for the sale
