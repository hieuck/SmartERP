# 🧪 Smart ERP - Beta Testing Program

## Program Overview

**Program Name**: Smart ERP Beta Testing Program  
**Duration**: 4 weeks  
**Participants**: 10 companies  
**Start Date**: Week 3 of launch plan  
**Incentive**: Free access for 3 months + priority support

---

## 🎯 Program Objectives

### Primary Objectives
1. **Validate Product-Market Fit**: Confirm Smart ERP solves real business problems
2. **Identify Bugs**: Find and fix critical issues before public launch
3. **Gather Feedback**: Collect user feedback to improve the product
4. **Build Testimonials**: Generate customer success stories
5. **Refine Onboarding**: Optimize the customer onboarding process

### Success Criteria
- 10 active beta users
- 80%+ satisfaction rate
- 5+ feature requests implemented
- 3+ testimonials collected
- 0 critical bugs at end of beta
- Average daily usage >30 minutes

---

## 👥 Beta Participant Profile

### Ideal Beta Candidate

**Company Characteristics:**
- **Industry**: Manufacturing, Trading, Wholesale, Retail
- **Size**: 10-50 employees
- **Revenue**: $500K - $10M annually
- **Location**: Vietnam (preferably Ho Chi Minh City or Hanoi for easy support)
- **Current System**: Spreadsheets or outdated software
- **Tech Savvy**: Comfortable with cloud solutions
- **Growth Stage**: Growing and looking to scale

**Decision Maker Characteristics:**
- **Role**: Owner, CEO, Operations Manager, IT Manager
- **Pain Points**: Manual processes, no visibility, inefficiency
- **Motivation**: Willing to try new solutions
- **Availability**: Can dedicate time for feedback
- **Communication**: Responsive and engaged

**Commitment Requirements:**
- Use Smart ERP for daily operations
- Provide weekly feedback
- Participate in feedback calls
- Report bugs and issues
- Provide testimonial if satisfied
- Available for case study (optional)

---

## 📋 Beta Program Structure

### Week 1: Recruitment & Onboarding

**Day 1-2: Recruitment**
- Identify 15 potential candidates (target 10 acceptances)
- Send personalized invitations
- Schedule intro calls
- Answer questions

**Day 3-5: Onboarding (First 5 Companies)**
- Conduct onboarding calls (1 hour each)
- Set up accounts
- Import initial data
- Provide training
- Set expectations

**Day 6-7: Onboarding (Next 5 Companies)**
- Repeat onboarding process
- Monitor first usage
- Provide immediate support
- Collect initial feedback

---

### Week 2: Active Usage & Support

**Daily:**
- Monitor system usage
- Respond to support requests within 2 hours
- Fix critical bugs immediately
- Track feature requests

**Mid-Week Check-in:**
- Email survey to all beta users
- Questions:
  - How often are you using Smart ERP?
  - What features are most valuable?
  - What challenges have you faced?
  - What would you improve?
  - Overall satisfaction (1-10)

**End of Week:**
- Review usage analytics
- Prioritize bug fixes
- Plan feature improvements
- Schedule 1-on-1 calls with active users

---

### Week 3: Optimization & Iteration

**Daily:**
- Continue monitoring and support
- Implement high-priority fixes
- Release updates
- Communicate changes

**Mid-Week:**
- Conduct 1-on-1 feedback calls (30 min each)
- Deep dive into their usage
- Understand pain points
- Identify success stories

**End of Week:**
- Release major improvements
- Send update announcement
- Collect feedback on improvements
- Start drafting case studies

---

### Week 4: Testimonials & Transition

**Daily:**
- Continue support
- Monitor satisfaction
- Prepare for soft launch

**Mid-Week:**
- Request testimonials from satisfied users
- Conduct case study interviews
- Offer extended free access
- Discuss transition to paid plans

**End of Week:**
- Final feedback survey
- Thank you gifts/recognition
- Transition plan to paid
- Beta program retrospective

## 📞 Beta Onboarding Call Script

**Duration**: 60 minutes

### Introduction (5 minutes)
> "Thank you for joining the Smart ERP Beta Program! I'm excited to help you get started.
> 
> Today we'll:
> 1. Set up your account
> 2. Import your data
> 3. Train your team
> 4. Answer your questions
> 
> Sound good? Let's dive in!"

### Discovery (10 minutes)
**Ask:**
- What's your biggest challenge with [current system]?
- How many people will use Smart ERP?
- What features are most important to you?
- What does success look like for you?

**Note their answers to tailor the demo and setup.**

### Account Setup (10 minutes)
- Create account
- Configure company settings
- Set up warehouses
- Create user accounts
- Assign roles

### Data Import (15 minutes)
- Explain import process
- Provide templates
- Help with first import (products)
- Verify data accuracy
- Schedule remaining imports

### Training (15 minutes)
- Dashboard overview
- Create a sample order
- Check inventory
- Generate a report
- Answer questions

### Expectations & Next Steps (5 minutes)
> "Here's what happens next:
> 1. You'll receive an email with login details and resources
> 2. Import your remaining data (we're here to help)
> 3. Start using Smart ERP for daily operations
> 4. We'll check in with you weekly
> 5. Report any issues immediately (we'll fix them fast)
> 
> Questions?"

### Feedback Process (5 minutes)
> "Your feedback is crucial! Here's how to share it:
> - **Bugs**: Email support@smarterp.com (we'll respond within 2 hours)
> - **Feature Requests**: Use the feedback form in the app
> - **General Feedback**: Weekly surveys (5 minutes)
> - **Deep Dives**: 1-on-1 calls (30 minutes, every 2 weeks)
> 
> We're building this for you, so please be honest!"

## 📊 Beta Feedback Collection

### Weekly Survey (5 minutes)

**Sent every Friday at 4 PM**

**Questions:**
1. How many days did you use Smart ERP this week? (0-7)
2. How satisfied are you with Smart ERP? (1-10)
3. What features did you use most? (checkboxes)
4. What challenges did you face? (open text)
5. What would you improve? (open text)
6. Any bugs to report? (open text)
7. Would you recommend Smart ERP to others? (Yes/No/Maybe)

---

### Bi-Weekly 1-on-1 Call (30 minutes)

**Scheduled every 2 weeks**

**Agenda:**
1. **Check-in** (5 min): How's it going overall?
2. **Usage Review** (10 min): Walk through their usage
3. **Pain Points** (5 min): What's frustrating?
4. **Feature Requests** (5 min): What's missing?
5. **Success Stories** (5 min): What's working well?

**Questions to Ask:**
- What tasks are you using Smart ERP for?
- What tasks are you still doing outside Smart ERP? Why?
- If you could change one thing, what would it be?
- What feature has saved you the most time?
- Would you pay for Smart ERP after the beta? Why/why not?

---

### End of Beta Survey (10 minutes)

**Sent at end of Week 4**

**Questions:**
1. Overall satisfaction with Smart ERP (1-10)
2. How likely are you to continue using Smart ERP? (1-10)
3. How likely are you to recommend Smart ERP? (1-10 - NPS)
4. What were the top 3 benefits you experienced?
5. What were the top 3 challenges you faced?
6. What features are most valuable to you?
7. What features are missing?
8. How would you rate the onboarding process? (1-10)
9. How would you rate the support? (1-10)
10. Would you be willing to provide a testimonial? (Yes/No)
11. Would you be interested in a case study? (Yes/No)
12. Any final feedback or suggestions?

## 🐛 Bug Reporting Process

### How Beta Users Report Bugs

**Method 1: Email**
- Send to: support@smarterp.com
- Subject: [BETA BUG] Brief description
- Include: Steps to reproduce, screenshots, expected vs actual behavior

**Method 2: In-App**
- Click "Report Bug" button
- Fill out form
- Automatically includes system info

**Method 3: Phone**
- Call: +84-XXX-XXX-XXXX
- Available: 9 AM - 6 PM

---

### Bug Triage Process

**Priority Levels:**

**P0 - Critical (Fix immediately)**
- System down
- Data loss
- Security vulnerability
- Blocking core functionality

**P1 - High (Fix within 24 hours)**
- Major feature broken
- Significant data issue
- Workaround available but difficult

**P2 - Medium (Fix within 1 week)**
- Minor feature broken
- UI issues
- Easy workaround available

**P3 - Low (Fix when possible)**
- Cosmetic issues
- Nice-to-have improvements
- Edge cases

---

### Bug Response SLA

| Priority | Response Time | Fix Time | Communication |
|----------|---------------|----------|---------------|
| **P0** | 30 minutes | 4 hours | Hourly updates |
| **P1** | 2 hours | 24 hours | Daily updates |
| **P2** | 4 hours | 1 week | Weekly updates |
| **P3** | 24 hours | Best effort | Monthly updates |
