Smart ERP is a modern, cloud-based Enterprise Resource Planning system designed for small and medium-sized businesses. It helps you manage inventory, sales, purchases, finances, and more—all in one platform.

### Who is Smart ERP for?
Smart ERP is perfect for:
- Small to medium manufacturing businesses (10-200 employees)
- Trading companies (import/export)
- Wholesale distributors
- Retail businesses with multiple locations
- Any business looking to move from spreadsheets to a professional system

### What makes Smart ERP different?
- **100% Test Coverage**: Enterprise-grade reliability
- **0 Security Vulnerabilities**: Bank-level security
- **Modern Technology**: Built with latest frameworks
- **Affordable Pricing**: 70% cheaper than competitors
- **Vietnamese Support**: Native language and local expertise
- **Fast Performance**: <100ms response time

### Is Smart ERP open source?
Yes, the core platform is open source. This means:

- Ability to customize for your needs
- No vendor lock-in

## Pricing & Plans

### How much does Smart ERP cost?
We offer four pricing tiers:
- **Free (Beta)**: $0/month - 1 user, 100 products, 50 orders/month
- **Starter**: $29/month - 5 users, 1,000 products, unlimited orders
- **Professional**: $99/month - 20 users, 10,000 products, advanced features
- **Enterprise**: Custom pricing - Unlimited users, all features, dedicated support

### Is there a free trial?
Yes! We offer a 30-day free trial for all paid plans. No credit card required to start.

### Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, there are no long-term contracts. You can cancel anytime with no penalties. If you cancel, you'll have access until the end of your billing period.

### What payment methods do you accept?
We accept:
- Credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, AMEX)
- Bank transfer
- Vietnamese payment methods (VNPay, MoMo)

### Do you offer discounts?
Yes! We offer:
- **Annual billing**: Save 2 months (pay for 10, get 12)
- **Non-profit discount**: 20% off for registered non-profits
- **Educational discount**: 30% off for schools and universities
- **Startup discount**: 50% off for first 6 months (for qualified startups)

### Are there any hidden fees?
No hidden fees! Our pricing is transparent. The only additional costs are:
- Extra users beyond your plan limit ($5/user/month)
- Extra storage beyond your plan limit ($10/100GB/month)
- Optional add-ons (custom integrations, training, etc.)

### What happens if I exceed my plan limits?
We'll notify you when you're approaching your limits. You can either:
- Upgrade to a higher plan
- Purchase add-ons for extra users/storage
- Remove unused data to stay within limits

We won't suddenly charge you or shut down your account.

## Troubleshooting

### I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
1. Go to login page
2. Click "Forgot Password"
3. Enter your email
4. Check email for reset link
5. Create new password

### I'm not receiving emails from Smart ERP
Check:
- Spam/junk folder
- Email address is correct in your profile
- Email provider isn't blocking us
- Contact support if issue persists

### The system is slow. What should I do?
Try:
- Refresh your browser
- Clear browser cache
- Check your internet connection
- Try a different browser
- Contact support if issue persists

### I'm getting an error message. What should I do?
1. Take a screenshot of the error
2. Note what you were doing when it occurred
3. Try refreshing the page
4. Contact support with details

### How do I report a bug?
To report a bug:
1. Email: support@smarterp.com
2. Include: Screenshots, steps to reproduce, browser/device info
3. We'll investigate and respond within 24 hours
